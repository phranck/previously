# Our own fork, with pull requests upstream but no waiting

We develop `phranck/bmnext` as a fork of `jhonnaker1/bmnext` and take changes from its `main` when they help us. Fixes of general use are offered upstream as pull requests, but nothing of ours waits for them to be merged. BMNeXT is one person's project, whose author works on five other bare metal emulators at the same time, and people who buy a Previously wait on us, not on it.
